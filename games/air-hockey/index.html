Air Hockey · 2 players

Knock the puck into the other player's goal (the colored slot on their wall).

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Knock the puck into the other player's goal (the colored slot on their wall).
Pink
W A S D to move. You defend the left goal.
Blue
Arrow keys to move. You defend the right goal.
Phone
Each player drags a finger on their own half. Best on a tablet.
Rules
You can't cross the middle line. Swing your paddle into the puck to hit it harder.
Win
First to 7 goals.
