Catch

Catch the falling snacks in your 🧺 basket.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Catch the falling snacks in your 🧺 basket.
Keys
← → to move.
Phone
Drag your finger.
Score
+1 per snack. Missing a snack is fine.
Lose
You have 3 lives. Every 💣 you catch costs one.
