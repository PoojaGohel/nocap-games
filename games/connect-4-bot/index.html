Connect 4 · vs bot

Get 4 PINK pieces in a row before the BLUE bot does: across, down, or diagonal.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Get 4 PINK pieces in a row before the BLUE bot does: across, down, or diagonal.
Controls
Tap a column, or aim with ← → and drop with space or ↓. You go first.
The bot
It wins when it can, blocks your 4-in-a-row, never sets up a win for you, and likes the middle.
Tip
Build two threats at once. It can only block one.
