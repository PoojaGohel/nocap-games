Connect 4 · 2 players

Get 4 of your pieces in a row: across, down, or diagonal.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Get 4 of your pieces in a row: across, down, or diagonal.
Turns
Pink and Blue take turns. The bottom line shows whose turn it is.
Keys
← → to aim, space or ↓ to drop.
Phone
Tap the column you want.
End
First to connect 4 wins. A full board with no winner is a draw.
