Copter

Fly through the cave as far as you can.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Fly through the cave as far as you can.
Controls
Hold space or ↑ (or hold the screen) to fly up. Let go to drop.
Danger
Touching the cave walls or the green blocks ends the run.
Harder
The cave gets narrower and faster the further you go.
