Flappy Dunk

Flap the 🏀 through every hoop. Missing a hoop ends the game.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Flap the 🏀 through every hoop. Missing a hoop ends the game.
Controls
Tap, or press space / ↑, to flap.
Score
+1 per hoop. Go through without touching the rim for a SWISH (+2). Swishes in a row build a 🔥 streak.
Rims
The red rims are solid. The ball bounces off them.
Lose
Missing a hoop, or hitting the floor.
