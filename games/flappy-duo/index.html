Flappy Duo · 2 players

Both birds fly through the same pipes. The bird that passes more pipes wins.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Both birds fly through the same pipes. The bird that passes more pipes wins.
Pink
A key, or tap the left half.
Blue
L key, or tap the right half.
Round
When both birds crash, the round ends. Flap to race again. Wins are counted at the top.
