Flappy

Fly through the gaps between the pipes.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Fly through the gaps between the pipes.
Keys
space or ↑ to flap.
Phone
Tap the screen.
Score
+1 for every pipe you pass. Your best score is saved until you press Restart.
Lose
Hit a pipe or the ground. Tap to try again.
Tip
Small, steady taps beat panic tapping.
