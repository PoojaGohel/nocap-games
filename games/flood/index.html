Flood

Make the whole board one color in 25 moves or fewer.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Make the whole board ONE color in 25 moves or fewer.
How
Your area starts in the top-left corner. Pick a color and your area turns that color, swallowing every touching square of that color.
Keys
Tap a color button, tap any square of that color, or press 1 to 6.
Tip
Look for the color that touches your area the most.
