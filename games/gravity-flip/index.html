Gravity Flip

Run as far as you can without hitting a spike.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Run as far as you can without hitting a spike.
Controls
Tap, or press space, to flip gravity. You fall up to the ceiling or down to the floor.
Rule
You can only flip while you are touching the floor or ceiling, so time it!
Harder
It speeds up, and later spikes come on both sides.
