Match 3

Reach the target score (green bar) before your 20 moves run out.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Reach the target score (green bar) before your 20 moves run out.
Swap
Tap a fruit, then tap the fruit next to it to swap them. Or press and swipe toward a neighbor.
Match
A swap only works if it makes a line of 3 or more of the same fruit. Otherwise they swap back for free.
Combos
Fruit falls after a match. New matches from falling fruit are combos and score double, triple and more.
Levels
Each level has a higher target. If there are no possible moves, the board reshuffles.
