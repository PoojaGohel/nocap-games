Math Rush

Solve each sum before the orange bar runs out.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Solve each sum before the orange bar runs out.
Controls
Tap the right answer, or press 1 2 3 4.
Levels
Every 5 right answers = level up. Level 1 is +, level 2 adds −, level 3 is ×, and level 4+ mixes everything including ÷.
Lose
A wrong answer or running out of time costs a ❤️. The right answer flashes green.
