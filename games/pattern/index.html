Pattern

Some squares light up blue. Remember them, then tap the exact same squares.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Some squares light up blue. Remember them, then tap the exact same squares.
Levels
Every level adds one more square. The grid grows from 3×3 up to 6×6.
Lose
Tapping a wrong square costs a ❤️ and gives you a new pattern. You have 3 hearts.
Tip
Look for shapes, like an L or a line, instead of single squares.
