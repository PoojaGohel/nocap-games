Racer

Drive as far as you can without crashing.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Drive as far as you can without crashing.
Keys
← → to switch lanes.
Phone
Tap the left or right side of the screen.
Score
Distance in meters. The road keeps getting faster.
Lose
Hit any car.
