Reversi · vs bot

Trap the bot's discs between yours to flip them. Most discs at the end wins.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Have more PINK discs than BLUE discs when the board is full.
Move
Place a disc so a line of the bot's discs is trapped between it and another of yours. The trapped discs flip to your color. The small dots show where you can go.
Keys
Tap a square, or move with the arrow keys and press space.
Skip
If you can't move, your turn is skipped. The game ends when nobody can move.
Bot
It loves corners and edges, and it counts how many discs each move flips.
