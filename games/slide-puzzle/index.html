Slide Puzzle

Slide the tiles back into order: 1, 2, 3... left to right, top to bottom, with the gap in the last corner.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Slide the tiles back into order: 1, 2, 3... left to right, top to bottom, with the gap in the last corner.
Controls
Tap any tile in the same row or column as the gap to slide it. Arrow keys slide a tile into the gap.
Help
Tiles turn green when they are in the right spot.
Levels
3×3, then 4×4, then 5×5. Try to use fewer moves!
