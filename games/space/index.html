Space

Shoot the 👾 aliens before they get past you.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Shoot the 👾 aliens before they get past you.
Keys
← → to move, space to shoot.
Phone
Hold and drag. Your ship fires on its own while you hold.
Score
+1 per alien. Aliens come faster over time.
Lose
You have 3 lives. Every alien that reaches the bottom costs one.
