Swing

Swing from rope to rope as far as you can without falling into the lava.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Swing from rope to rope as far as you can without falling into the lava.
Controls
Hold the screen or space to grab the nearest 📍 in front of you. Let go to fly.
Start
You begin on a rope. Press and hold, then let go at the top of the swing.
Tip
Let go while swinging forward and up, then grab the next rope while you are still flying.
Score
Distance, plus 20 for every 🍌.
