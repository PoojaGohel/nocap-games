Target Rush

Hit as many targets as you can in 30 seconds.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Hit as many targets as you can in 30 seconds.
Points
Targets grow, then shrink away. Hit them while they're small for more points.
Combo
Hits in a row build a combo. A missed tap or a vanished target resets it.
Keys
Tap or click the targets.
