Tic Tac Toe · 2 players

Get 3 of your marks in a row before the other player: across, down, or diagonal.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Get 3 of your marks in a row: across, down, or diagonal.
Turns
PINK is ❌, BLUE is ⭕. Pass the device after each move.
Keys
Tap a square, or move with ← ↑ ↓ → and press space.
Fair
The starting player swaps every round.
Next
Tap after a game to play again. Wins are counted at the top.
