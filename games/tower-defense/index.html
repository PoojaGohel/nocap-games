Tower Defense

Stop the bugs from walking all the way to the 🏁. You have 10 ❤️.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Stop the bugs from walking all the way to the 🏁. You have 10 ❤️.
Build
Tap the grass (not the path) to build a 🗼 tower for 50 🪙.
Upgrade
Tap a tower to upgrade it (more damage, range and speed). Max level is ⭐⭐⭐.
Coins
Every bug you squash pays coins. 🦗 crickets are fast, 🐌 snails are tough.
Waves
There is a short break between waves. Tap ▶ next wave (or press space) to start early.
Tip
Build where the path bends. A tower there can hit bugs on two parts of the path.
