Tug of War · 2 players

Pull the 🚩 flag all the way to your side.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Pull the 🚩 flag all the way to your side.
Pink
Mash A, or keep tapping the left half.
Blue
Mash L, or keep tapping the right half.
How
Wait for 3-2-1. Every press pulls the flag one step toward you.
Win
The flag reaches your colored zone. Press or tap again for a rematch.
