Word Guess

Find the hidden word one letter at a time before you run out of hearts.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Find the hidden word before you run out of ❤️.
Hint
The emoji above the word tells you what kind of thing it is.
Keys
Type a letter on your keyboard, or tap one on the screen.
Lives
Every wrong letter costs a ❤️. You have 6.
Streak
Guess words in a row to build your streak 🔥.
