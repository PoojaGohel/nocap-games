Zigzag

Keep the ball on the twisting path as long as you can.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Keep the ball on the twisting path as long as you can.
Controls
Tap, or press space, to switch direction (↖ or ↗).
Timing
Tap right at each corner. Too early or too late and the ball rolls off the edge.
Score
Distance, plus 5 for every 💎. The ball keeps speeding up.
