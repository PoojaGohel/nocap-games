2048

Merge tiles to make a 2048 tile.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Merge tiles to make a 2048 tile.
Keys
Arrow keys, or the buttons under the screen.
Phone
Swipe up, down, left or right.
How
Every move slides all tiles. Two touching tiles with the same number merge into one. A new 2 or 4 appears after each move.
Score
Each merge adds the new tile's value to your score.
Lose
The board is full and nothing can merge.
