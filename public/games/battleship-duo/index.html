Battleship · 2 players

Pass and play. Sink all 4 of the other player's ships before they sink yours.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Sink all 4 of the other player's ships (12 squares) before they sink yours.
Pass
Each player sets up their fleet in secret. A cover screen hides the boards every time you pass the device.
Setup
Tap 🔀 (or R) to shuffle your ships, then ✅ (or space) when you like it.
Fire
Tap a square on ENEMY WATERS, or aim with the arrows and press space. 💥 = hit, dot = miss.
Ships
Battleship 4 · cruiser 3 · sub 3 · speedboat 2. Sunk ships turn dark red.
