Battleship · vs bot

Sink all 4 enemy ships (12 squares) before the bot sinks yours.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Sink all 4 enemy ships (12 squares) before the bot sinks yours.
Fire
Tap a square on ENEMY WATERS (right). 💥 = hit, dot = miss. You and the bot take turns.
Your fleet
The left board shows your ships and where the bot fired (🔥 = hit).
The bot
It searches in a checkerboard pattern. Once it hits, it fires around that spot.
Ships
Battleship 4 · cruiser 3 · sub 3 · speedboat 2. Sunk ships turn dark red.
