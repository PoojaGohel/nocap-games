Bounce

Keep the ball in the air. Don't let it touch the floor.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Keep the ball in the air. Don't let it touch the floor.
Kick
Tap the ball to kick it up. Hit it on the left to send it right, and the other way round.
Keys
Press space when the ball is in the pink kick zone at the bottom.
Stars
Bounce the ball through a ⭐ for +5.
Harder
The ball gets heavier as your score goes up.
