Bricks

Break all 32 bricks with the ball.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Break all 32 bricks with the ball.
Keys
← → to move the paddle.
Phone
Drag your finger.
Score
+10 per brick. Where the ball hits the paddle changes its angle.
Lose
You have 3 lives. Letting the ball fall costs one.
