Dots & Boxes · 2 players

Draw lines between dots. Close a box to own it and go again.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Own more boxes than the other player when the board is full.
Turns
PINK and BLUE take turns drawing one line between two dots.
Bonus
Close the 4th side of a box and it's yours, and you go again.
Keys
Tap a gap between two dots, or move with the arrow keys and press space.
Tip
Don't draw the 3rd side of a box. The other player will take it.
