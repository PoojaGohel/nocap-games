Drop Merge

Drop number tiles and merge them into bigger and bigger numbers.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Drop number tiles and merge them into bigger and bigger numbers.
Controls
Tap a column to drop. Or aim with ← → and drop with space or ↓.
Merge
When a tile touches the same number (left, right, above or below) they merge into double. Three 8s together = 32!
Chains
After a merge, tiles fall and can merge again. Chains are worth big points.
Lose
A full column only takes a tile that matches its top. When no column can take the next tile, it is game over. Check NEXT to plan ahead.
