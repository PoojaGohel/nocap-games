Emoji Recall

Watch the emojis flash one at a time, then tap them back in the same order.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Watch the emojis flash one at a time, then tap them back in the same order.
Controls
Tap the emojis at the bottom, or press 1 to 8.
Levels
Starts with 3 emojis. Every level adds one more and flashes a little faster.
Lose
One wrong pick costs a ❤️ and shows the right answer. You have 3 hearts.
