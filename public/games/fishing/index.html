Fishing

Catch as many points of fish as you can in 60 seconds.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Catch as many points of fish as you can in 60 seconds.
Controls
Hold space or ↓ (or hold the screen) to drop the hook. Let go to reel up. ← → move the boat.
Phone
Hold where you want to fish. The boat follows your finger.
Points
🐟 1 · 🐠 3 · 🐡 5 · 🦑 10 (deep and fast) · 🥾 0
Danger
A 🦈 shark steals the fish off your hook if it touches it.
