Hockey · vs bot

Score 7 goals in the bot's goal (right side) before it scores 7 in yours.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Score 7 goals in the bot's goal (right side) before it scores 7 in yours.
Controls
Drag anywhere on the screen, or use the arrow keys / W A S D. You stay on the left half.
The bot
It defends its goal, then rushes the puck when it crosses the middle. It speeds up when you are winning.
Tip
Hit the puck at an angle off the walls. The bot is slow to react to bank shots.
