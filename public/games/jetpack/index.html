Jetpack

Fly as far as you can and grab 🪙 coins. Each coin is worth 10 points.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Fly as far as you can and grab 🪙 coins. Each coin is worth 10 points.
Controls
Hold space or ↑ (or hold the screen) to fly up. Let go to fall.
Zappers
The ⚡ yellow laser bars come in different angles. Touching one ends the run.
Missiles
⚠️ means a 🚀 missile is locking on to your height. Move away before it fires!
