Jump

Run as far as you can and jump over 🌵 cacti and 🪨 rocks.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Run as far as you can and jump over 🌵 cacti and 🪨 rocks.
Keys
space or ↑ to jump.
Phone
Tap the screen.
Score
Goes up the longer you run. The speed keeps rising.
Lose
Touch an obstacle. Tap to run again.
