Light Bikes · 2 players

Your bike never stops and leaves a glowing trail. Make the other player crash.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Your bike never stops and leaves a glowing trail. Make the other player crash.
Pink
W A S D to turn.
Blue
Arrow keys to turn.
Phone
Tap your half of the screen in the direction you want to go.
Lose
Hit a wall, your own trail, or the other trail. If both crash at once, it's a draw.
Rounds
Press space or tap for the next round. Wins are counted at the top.
