Lights Out

Turn every light OFF.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Turn every light OFF.
How
Tapping a light flips it AND the lights directly above, below, left and right of it.
Stars
Every puzzle can be solved in "par" taps. Finish in par for ⭐⭐⭐.
Stuck?
Tap ↺ reset (or press R) to start the level over.
Tip
Try clearing the top row first, then use the row below to fix the row above.
