Maze

Get your 🐸 from the top left to the 🚪 exit at the bottom right.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Get your 🐸 from the top left to the 🚪 exit at the bottom right.
Keys
Arrow keys or the buttons. Hold a key to keep walking.
Phone
Tap on the side you want to move toward.
Points
⭐ = 50 points. Escaping faster gives a bigger speed bonus.
Levels
Every level is a brand new random maze, and bigger. From level 4 you can only see around you 🔦.
