Memory

Find all the matching emoji pairs, then level up.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Find all the matching emoji pairs, then level up.
How
Tap or click two cards. A match stays face up. If they don't match, they flip back.
Peek
Each level starts by showing every card so you can memorize them. Bigger levels give you more time.
Levels
6 levels: 6, 8, 12, 16, 20, then 24 cards. Memorize time: 4, 6, 9, 12, 15, then 18 seconds.
Stars
⭐⭐⭐ for finishing in about one move per pair. Collect up to 18 stars across all 6 levels.
Next
Tap or press space after a level to start the next one.
