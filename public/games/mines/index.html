Mines

Open every square that doesn't have a bomb. There are 15 bombs.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Open every square that doesn't have a bomb. There are 15 bombs.
Open
Tap or click a square. Your first tap is always safe.
Flag
Hold a square to plant a 🚩, or turn on flag mode (top right, or press F).
Numbers
A number tells you how many bombs touch that square, including diagonals.
Lose
Open a bomb. Win by opening all the safe squares.
