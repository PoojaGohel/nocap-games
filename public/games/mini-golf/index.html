Mini Golf

Get the ball into the hole by the 🚩 in as few shots as you can. 5 holes.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Get the ball into the hole by the 🚩 in as few shots as you can. 5 holes.
Shoot
Press anywhere, pull back like a slingshot, and let go. Pull further = harder shot.
Keys
← → to aim, ↑ ↓ for power, space to shoot.
Walls
The ball bounces off walls and edges. Use bank shots!
Score
Each hole has a par (a normal number of shots). Under par is great: birdie (-1), eagle (-2), hole in one!
Tip
A ball that is going too fast rolls right over the hole.
