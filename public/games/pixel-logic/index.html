Pixel Logic

Use the number clues to fill in the hidden picture.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Find the hidden picture by filling in the right squares.
Clues
The numbers by each row and column are the runs of filled squares in that line, in order. "3 1" means 3 filled, a gap, then 1 more.
Tap
Tap a square to fill it. Switch to ❌ mode to mark squares you know are empty.
Keys
Arrows to move, space to fill, X to mark.
Levels
Pictures get bigger as you solve them, from 5 × 5 up to 8 × 8.
