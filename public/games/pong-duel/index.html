Pong Duel · 2 players

Hit the ball past the other player's paddle to score.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Hit the ball past the other player's paddle to score.
Pink
W up, S down. Left paddle.
Blue
↑ up, ↓ down. Right paddle.
Phone
Each player drags a finger on their own half of the screen.
Win
First to 7 points. The ball gets faster with every hit.
Tip
Hit the ball with the edge of your paddle for a sharp angle.
