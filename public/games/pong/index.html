Pong

Get the ball past the bot's paddle on the right.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Get the ball past the bot's paddle on the right.
Keys
↑ ↓ to move.
Phone
Drag your finger.
Win
First to 5. The ball speeds up with every hit.
Tip
Hit with the edge of your paddle. The bot is slow on sharp angles.
