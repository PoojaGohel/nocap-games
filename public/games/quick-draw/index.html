Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Wait for the screen to turn green and say GO!, then press first.
Pink
A key, or tap the left half.
Blue
L key, or tap the right half.
Careful
Press before GO! and the other player gets the point.
Score
One point per round. Your reaction time shows in milliseconds.
