Rock Paper Scissors · vs bot

Beat the bot to 10 wins. Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, paper beats rock.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Beat the bot to 10 wins. Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, paper beats rock.
Controls
Tap a hand, or press R P S (or 1 2 3).
The bot
It remembers what you usually play after each hand, and plays whatever beats its guess. It tells you what it guessed.
Tip
Humans fall into patterns. Try to be random, or set a trap and switch it up.
Rematch
Tap after a match. The bot keeps its memory of you.
