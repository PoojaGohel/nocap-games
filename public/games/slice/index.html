Slice

Swipe through the flying fruit to slice it.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Swipe through the flying fruit to slice it.
Controls
Drag fast with your finger, or hold the mouse button and swipe. Tap to start.
Combo
Slice 3 or more fruits in one swipe for bonus points. COMBO x4 = +4.
Power-ups
❄️ slows everything down for 3 seconds. ❤️ gives you an extra life (up to 5).
Levels
Every 10 points you level up: more fruit, flying faster, and more bombs.
Lose
Slicing a 💣 ends the game right away. Every fruit that falls costs a ❤️.
