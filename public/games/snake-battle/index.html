Snake Battle · vs bot

Survive longer than the 🤖 bot snake. Eat 🍎 to grow.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Survive longer than the 🤖 bot snake. Eat 🍎 to grow.
Keys
Arrow keys, or the buttons under the screen.
The bot
It heads for the apple and avoids spots with no room to escape.
Lose
Hit a wall, yourself, or the bot. Head-on crash = draw.
Rounds
Tap or press space for the next round. Wins are counted at the top.
