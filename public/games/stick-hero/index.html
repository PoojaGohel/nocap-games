Stick Hero

Get the 🥷 from platform to platform.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Get the 🥷 from platform to platform.
Controls
Hold the screen or space to grow the stick. Let go and it falls over to make a bridge.
Rule
The stick has to land on the next platform. Too short or too long, and you fall.
Perfect
Land the stick right on the red dot for +1 bonus.
Harder
Platforms get thinner as your score goes up.
