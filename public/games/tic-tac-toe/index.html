Tic Tac Toe

Get 3 ❌ in a row before the ⭕ bot does: across, down, or diagonal.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Get 3 ❌ in a row before the ⭕ bot does: across, down, or diagonal.
Controls
Click or tap a square. You always go first.
Bot
It takes a win when it can and blocks yours.
Tip
Set up two ways to win at once. The bot can only block one.
Next
Tap after a game to play again.
