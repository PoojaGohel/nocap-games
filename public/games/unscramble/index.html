Unscramble

The letters of a Gen Z word are mixed up. Put them back in order. CIRZZ → RIZZ.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
The letters of a Gen Z word are mixed up. Put them back in order. CIRZZ → RIZZ.
Controls
Tap the yellow letters in order, or type them on a keyboard. Backspace or ⌫ undoes.
Buttons
🔀 mixes the letters again. 💡 fills in the next letter but costs 5 points.
Score
10 points per letter. Words get longer as you solve more. You have 90 seconds.
