Zombies

Survive the zombie waves as long as you can.

Keyboard works anywhere on the page. On a phone, tap the screen or use the buttons.

How to play

Goal
Survive the zombie waves as long as you can.
Move
W A S D or arrow keys. On a phone, hold and drag where to walk.
Shoot
Automatic! You always fire at the closest zombie.
Power-ups
⚡ = rapid fire for 5 seconds · 💊 = +1 ❤️ (up to 5). Zombies sometimes drop them.
Waves
A new wave every 15 seconds: more and faster zombies. Big zombies take 3 hits.
Lose
Each bite costs a ❤️. You blink and are safe for a moment after a bite.
